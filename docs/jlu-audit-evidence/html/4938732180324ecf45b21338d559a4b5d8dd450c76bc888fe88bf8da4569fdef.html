Application for Open Projects (2025)

09/08/2025 | 梁 辉 | Events | Views:2467

According to the “Measures for the Administration of National Key Laboratories” (Jiaoke Xin Si [2025] No. 46), combined with the specific circumstances of the National Key Laboratory of Inorganic Synthesis and Preparation Chemistry (hereinafter referred to as “the Laboratory”), open topic applications are being implemented for researchers. The Laboratory mainly engages in basic research and applied foundational research such as inorganic synthesis methodologies, theories related to inorganic synthesis, and the development of new inorganic materials. It operates under a mechanism of “openness, mobility, collaboration, and competition.” Through the implementation of open topics, it aims to organize high-level basic and applied foundational research, gather and cultivate outstanding scientific talents, promote interdisciplinary integration, and facilitate high-level academic exchanges. For application details this year, please refer to the attached specific content.
